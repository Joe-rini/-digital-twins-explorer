# Digital twins are funhouse mirrors — the machinery

Interactive walkthrough of Peng, Brucks, Gui et al., *Digital twins are funhouse mirrors: five
systematic distortions*, Science Advances 12(36), eaeh8260, 2 September 2026.
https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/sciadv.aeh8260

**Live page:** [https://USERNAME.github.io/REPO/](https://joe-rini.github.io/-digital-twins-explorer/)

Shows the mechanics of the study using the real artefacts: the survey participants took, the prompt
the models received, and every model answer beside the human one — followed through one participant's
complete record.

## Attribution

The survey, human answers and 21 of the 29 model arms are reproduced from the research team's public
release (paper CC BY 4.0). Data: https://huggingface.co/datasets/LLM-Digital-Twin/Twin-2K-500-Mega-Study
· Code: https://github.com/TianyiPeng/Twin-2K-500-Mega-Study

Eight arms — Claude Haiku 4.5 and Sonnet 5 at four persona depths — are not the authors' and were
added afterwards. Participant identified only by the study's anonymous twin ID.

`index.html` is self-contained apart from Google Fonts.
